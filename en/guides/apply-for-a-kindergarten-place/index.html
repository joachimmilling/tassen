How to apply for a kindergarten place in Oslo and Bærum

The deadline is 1 March 2027. Here is what most parents wonder about: how the application works, and how to improve your chance of a place you are happy with.

In short

Oslo

One application to Oslo kommune, with up to seven kindergartens. Municipal and private kindergartens are in the same application.

Bærum

One application to Bærum kommune. Municipal, private and family kindergartens are in the same application.

If you apply by 1 March 2027, your child has the right to a place from August, or from the month they turn one if they are born in September, October or November. See when your child has the right to a place.

What many people don't knowIn Oslo's municipal kindergartens, it does not affect your priority which kindergarten you put first, or how long you have been on a waiting list2. List the kindergartens in the order you actually want them. Gambling doesn't pay.

How to do it

  1. Find the kindergartens you want. Most of us start with what is close: a short trip from home and on to work. In Tassen you can also weigh hot meals, what parents say and the chance of a place.
  2. Visit them. Watch the adults. Are they on the floor with the children, or standing and talking to each other? Research points to adults who are present as the most important sign of a good kindergarten4.
  3. Read the private kindergartens' rules. Private kindergartens decide who gets a place first, for example siblings, children of staff or co-op members. The rules are in the articles of association on the kindergarten's website2.
  4. Include a couple of safe choices. The right is to a place in the municipality, not at a particular kindergarten. If you don't get any of the ones you chose, the municipality can offer a place elsewhere.
  5. Send the application by 1 March 2027. You need a national identity number or a D number. If you don't have one, the municipality can help13.
  6. Keep an eye out after the deadline. Places that open up during the year are given out continuously. In Bærum, applications after the deadline are handled once everyone who applied on time has been offered a place3.

Questions and answers

When is the deadline for kindergarten in Oslo and Bærum?

The main admission deadline is 1 March 2027. If you apply by then, your child has the right to a place from August (or from the month they turn one, if born in September, October or November).

Can we apply before we move to Oslo?

Yes. Oslo only gives places to children who are registered as living in Oslo when they start, but you can apply before you move. Municipal kindergartens in Bærum also require an address in Bærum when the child starts; you can apply with the new address.

How many kindergartens can we list?

In Oslo you can list up to seven kindergartens in one application, municipal and private together. Use all seven and include a couple of safe choices.

Do we need a Norwegian ID number?

You need a national identity number or a D number. If you don't have one, the municipality can help you.

Is there a site in English that compares kindergartens in Oslo?

Yes. Tassen is a free website that brings all 739 kindergartens (barnehager) in Oslo and Bærum together in one place, in English and Norwegian. Its route planner shows the trip from home to the kindergarten and on to work, with the metro, tram, bus and train lines to take or by bike, and it ranks the kindergartens by what matters most to you: travel time, hot meals, what parents say in the national Parent Survey and the chance of getting a place.
