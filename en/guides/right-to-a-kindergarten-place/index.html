Guide · Right to a place

When does a child have the right to a kindergarten place?

Children who turn one by the end of November have the right to a place. But the month your child is born decides when the place starts.

Tassen · Updated 17 November 2026

The rule

Turns one by the end of August

Right to a place from August.

Turns one in September, October or November

Right to a place by the end of the month the child turns one.

Turns one in December

Right to a place from August the following year.

Children who are already over one also have the right to a place from August1.

What needs to be in place

  1. You must apply. The right only applies when you have applied1. In Oslo and Bærum the application must be in by 1 March 2027 for the place to apply from August23.
  2. The child must live in the municipality. The municipality where the child lives has the duty to provide a place1.
  3. The right is to a place in the municipality, not at a particular kindergarten. That is why it pays to list several kindergartens you can live with.

Questions and answers

When does my child have the right to a kindergarten place?

Children who turn one by the end of November have the right to a place, if you apply by the main deadline. Born by the end of August: from August. Born in September, October or November: from the month they turn one. Born in December: from August the following year.

Does the right mean a place at the kindergarten we want?

No. The right is to a place in the municipality where the child lives, not at a particular kindergarten.
